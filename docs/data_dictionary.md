# Data dictionary

One row = one payment attempt. 180,000 rows × 20 columns, covering 1 January – 30 June 2025.

> **All data is synthetic**, generated by [`../src/generate_data.py`](../src/generate_data.py). PayPulse, its banks, merchants and customers are fictional. No PhonePe data or data from any other real company is used.

---

## Fields

| # | Field | Type | Business meaning | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `transaction_id` | Text | Unique identifier for one payment attempt | `TXN000012345` | `TXN` + 9 digits, always unique |

| 5 | `amount_inr` | Decimal | Attempted payment value in INR | `499.00` | Range ₹10 – ₹75,000; median ₹314.78, mean ₹688.40 |
| 6 | `payment_method` | Category | Instrument used | `UPI` | 5 values, see below |
| 7 | `issuing_bank` | Category | Fictional issuing bank of the instrument | `Crescent Bank` | 6 values; none maps to a real bank |
| 8 | `merchant_category` | Category | Merchant industry grouping | `Grocery` | 8 values |
| 9 | `city` | Category | City of the attempt | `Pune` | 12 Indian cities |
| 10 | `state` | Category | State of the attempt | `Maharashtra` | 11 states |
| 11 | `region` | Category | Region of the attempt | `West` | North, South, East, West, Northeast |
| 12 | `device_type` | Category | Device the attempt came from | `Android` | Android, iOS, Web |
| 13 | `network_type` | Category | Connection quality at attempt time | `4G` | WiFi, 5G, 4G, 3G, 2G |
| 14 | `customer_tenure_group` | Category | How long the customer has been on the platform | `1-3 years` | 4 bands |
| 15 | `transaction_status` | Category | **Outcome of the initial attempt** | `Failed` | `Success` or `Failed`; the success-rate KPI is built on this |

| 20 | `final_retry_outcome` | Category | Result after retrying | `Recovered` | `Not Retried`, `Recovered`, `Still Failed` |

## Derived fields (`transaction_enriched` view)

Created by [`../src/build_database.py`](../src/build_database.py) from constants in `config.py`, so the definitions cannot drift from the ones used elsewhere.

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| `transaction_date` | `date(transaction_timestamp)` |
| `week_start_date` | Monday-anchored week start, so weekly charts plot as real dates |
| `transaction_month` | `YYYY-MM` |
| `transaction_hour` | Hour of day, 0–23 |
| `peak_period` | `Peak` when the hour is 18–22 inclusive, else `Non-peak` |

| `incident_period` | `Before` / `During` / `After` relative to 10–20 March 2025 |
| `bank_group` | `Crescent Bank` or `All other banks`, for the incident control comparison |

## Reading conventions

**`transaction_status` is the initial attempt.** A row with `transaction_status = 'Failed'` and `final_retry_outcome = 'Recovered'` means the first attempt failed and a retry succeeded. The headline success rate counts that row as a failure; the retry recovery rate counts it as a recovery. Both are reported, and neither is blended into the other.

**A blank `failure_reason` is meaningful, not missing.** It is blank exactly when `transaction_status = 'Success'`. `validate_data.py` asserts this in both directions and excludes the column from the completeness check.

**Latency is not uniform across outcomes.** Successful attempts average 1.57 s, failed attempts 4.77 s, and Bank Timeout failures 8.04 s. That is why the P95 (3.44 s) sits so far above the mean (1.71 s), and why tail latency is reported rather than only the average.

**Amounts are long-tailed.** Median ₹314.78 against a ₹688.40 mean, with 499 attempts above ₹10,000. Segments built on the top band are flagged `low_sample_flag = 1` in `segment_performance` and are not used for recommendations.

## Analysis outputs

The SQL layer writes 16 tables to [`../outputs/analysis/`](../outputs/analysis/), all committed:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `kpi_overview.csv` | One-row leadership scorecard |
| `monthly_kpis.csv` | Monthly volume, value, success rate, MoM movement |
| `daily_trend.csv` | Daily series with a 7-day moving average and DoD change |
| `weekly_trend.csv` | Weekly series with WoW change and a complete-week flag |
| `failure_pareto.csv` | Failures by cause with rank, share and cumulative share |
| `peak_comparison.csv` | Peak vs non-peak, including timeout share of failures |
| `hourly_performance.csv` | 24-hour volume/reliability profile with worst-hour rank |
| `bank_performance.csv` | Bank scorecard with 95% CI half-width and gap to portfolio |
| `segment_performance.csv` | 8 dimensions, with contribution, rank and low-sample flag |
| `bank_hour_impact.csv` | Top 20 bank × hour cells ranked by absolute failures |
| `retry_analysis.csv` | Retry coverage and recovery per failure cause |
| `retry_by_attempt.csv` | Recovery rate by retry ordinal |
| `incident_analysis.csv` | Before/during/after, normalised per day |
| `incident_bank_comparison.csv` | Affected bank vs all other banks, peak hours only |
| `impact_baseline.csv` | Targeted failure pools with average failed value |
| `simulator_baseline.csv` | The five mutually exclusive pools the simulator uses |
| `summary.json` | Every headline metric and all three scenarios |
